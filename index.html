Dairy Queen vs Other Fast-Food Desserts: What Is on the Menu

This is an unofficial informational guide and is not affiliated with Dairy Queen. It compares Dairy Queen desserts with other fast-food desserts in simple words, so you can see what makes each one different. For the full list, see the Dairy Queen menu.

Dairy Queen Blizzards

A Blizzard is soft serve blended with candy, cookies or fruit. It is thick, cold and filling. Other fast-food places often serve a milkshake or a sundae instead, but a Blizzard is different because the mix-ins are blended right through the ice cream, not just on top.

Classic Blizzard flavours include Oreo, Reese's Peanut Butter Cup, M&M's and cookie dough. Seasonal flavours change through the year. A Mini size is a good way to try one without ordering a large cup.

Sundaes and Shakes

A sundae is soft serve with a topping on top, such as hot fudge, strawberry or caramel. Most fast-food places serve a similar sundae, but Dairy Queen soft serve is known for being smooth and creamy.

A shake is soft serve blended with milk and a flavour, served with a straw. It is smooth and easy to drink. Other places also serve shakes, but the flavour range can be smaller. Dairy Queen shakes are often available in vanilla, chocolate, strawberry and other flavours, depending on the location.

Cones and Simple Treats

A cone is the simplest dessert: soft serve in a crisp cone. Most fast-food places offer a cone or a cup of soft serve. Dairy Queen cones are popular because they are quick, cheap and easy to eat on the go.

Other places may offer a frozen yoghurt or a slushie instead. Those are lighter, but they do not have the same creamy taste as soft serve.

Ice Cream Cakes

Dairy Queen ice cream cakes are different from most fast-food desserts. They combine cake, ice cream and a crunchy layer in the middle, and they serve several people at once. Other fast-food places do not usually sell a full ice cream cake to take home.

An ice cream cake is best for birthdays and family gatherings. You need to keep it frozen until serving time, and let it sit for a few minutes before cutting it.

What Other Places Offer

Other fast-food desserts are often simpler. Many places offer a small sundae, a shake or a cookie. Some offer a pie or a brownie. These are good for a quick sweet treat, but they do not have the same range as Dairy Queen.

Dairy Queen has a wider dessert menu, with Blizzards, sundaes, shakes, cones, cakes and more. That range is useful if you are ordering for a group with different tastes.

Which Dessert Should You Choose?

If you want something thick and filling, choose a Blizzard. If you want something light, choose a sundae or a cone. If you want something to drink, choose a shake. If you are celebrating, choose an ice cream cake.

Think about price too. A cone is usually the cheapest dessert. A Blizzard or a cake costs more, but it serves more or gives you more flavour. Prices vary by location, so check the menu board at your local restaurant.
